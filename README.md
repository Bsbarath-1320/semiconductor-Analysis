# 🔬 Semiconductor Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing semiconductor purchase, sales,
supplier, product, and shipping data using Power BI.

The dashboard transforms semiconductor business data into meaningful
insights and helps understand purchase performance, sales performance,
supplier contribution, product performance, and transportation analysis.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze semiconductor purchase performance
- Analyze product-wise sales
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze supplier-wise purchase and sales
- Compare purchase quantity across products
- Analyze units sold for each product
- Understand transportation methods
- Analyze shipping status
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

## 📈 Dashboard Modules

### 1. Purchase Analysis

The Purchase Analysis section provides insights into:

- Top 3 Purchase Products
- Total Purchase Quantity by Product
- Purchase Amount by Supplier
- Product-wise Purchase Performance
- Supplier-wise Purchase Contribution

### 2. Sales Analysis

The Sales Analysis section includes:

- Total Sales
- Top 3 Sales Products
- Units Sold by Product
- Supplier-wise Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Product-wise Sales Performance

### 3. Portway Analysis

The Portway Analysis section analyzes transportation methods:

- Airways
- Seaways
- Roadways
- Sales by Portable Way
- Shipping Status
- Products under different shipping statuses

### 4. Shipping Analysis

Shipping performance is analyzed using different statuses:

- In Transit
- Delayed at Customs
- Delivered
- Dispatched
- Pending

## 🔍 Key Insights

- AI Accelerator GPU is one of the leading products in sales analysis.
- Silicon Wafer 300mm has the highest purchase quantity among the
